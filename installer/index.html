Install Enigma M3

Before you install

  1. Back up the firmware and settings you want to keep. Installing Enigma replaces the current firmware and can overwrite saved settings.
  2. Connect your Cardputer-ADV, click Install Enigma, then select its USB port and confirm the installation. Leave full flash erase unchecked if offered.
  3. Keep the cable connected until installation finishes. If the device stays in download mode, reset it with G0 released.

The installer detects the ESP32-S3 chip. Check the model on your device before selecting its USB port.

Connection help

The Cardputer does not appear

Try a USB-C data cable and plug it directly into your computer. To enter download mode, switch the Cardputer off, unplug USB, hold G0, and reconnect USB. Release G0 when the device powers on, then try again.

The port is busy or flashing fails

Close M5Burner, serial monitors, and other tabs using the device. Reconnect the Cardputer in download mode and try again. If your browser cannot access the serial port, check your system’s USB serial permissions.

My browser cannot install firmware

Use a desktop browser with Web Serial enabled and open the site over HTTPS. In Brave, check Serial ports in the site settings. For a local preview, use localhost or 127.0.0.1. You can also download the merged image below and use an ESP32-S3 flasher with offset 0x0.
